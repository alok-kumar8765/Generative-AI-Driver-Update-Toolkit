🚀💻 HOW TO CREATE A BOOTABLE WinPE USB — EVERY IT TECHNICIAN SHOULD KNOW THIS! 🛠️

Imagine a computer that refuses to boot into Windows. 😰 Your client's files are important, and reinstalling Windows could put their data at risk. What do you do?

The solution? A properly prepared WinPE USB! 🔥

A bootable Windows PE (WinPE) USB gives you a lightweight Windows environment to troubleshoot computers, recover important files, diagnose problems, and perform system repairs without loading the installed Windows operating system.

🛠️ WHAT YOU NEED:
✅ A USB drive (8GB or larger recommended).
✅ A legitimate WinPE image or a WinPE build created with Microsoft's Windows ADK and WinPE add-on.
✅ A computer to prepare the USB.
✅ A suitable USB creation tool, depending on the image format.

💡 TECHNICIAN TIPS:
🔹 Back up your USB files first — creating bootable media may erase everything on it.
🔹 Use GPT for most modern UEFI computers; older BIOS systems may require MBR.
🔹 Test your USB before an emergency happens.
🔹 Always protect client data and obtain permission before accessing files.

🔥 Remember: A good technician doesn't wait for a computer emergency before preparing recovery tools!
