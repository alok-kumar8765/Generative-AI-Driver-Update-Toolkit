🚀 NO INTERNET? SLOW CONNECTION? NETWORK ERRORS?

💻 Don't panic! Your Windows PC might just need a quick network repair.

With a few powerful PowerShell commands, you can troubleshoot common network problems, flush your DNS cache, renew your IP address, reset Winsock, and repair your TCP/IP network stack. ⚡🌐

🔥 Some useful commands to know:
✅ "ipconfig /flushdns" — Clear the DNS cache
✅ "ipconfig /renew" — Renew your IP address
✅ "netsh winsock reset" — Reset Winsock
✅ "netsh int ip reset" — Reset TCP/IP
✅ "ping 8.8.8.8" — Test connectivity

💡 IT TIP: Run PowerShell as Administrator, save your work, and restart your computer after completing the network resets. Remember, some commands may temporarily disconnect your network.
